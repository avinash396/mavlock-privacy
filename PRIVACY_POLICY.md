# MavLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11-11-2026

MavLock ("the app") is an offline password manager. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect
Nothing. MavLock does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no
internet permission, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.

## Where your data is stored
All passwords and notes you save stay in the app's private storage on your device, encrypted with
AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is protected by your master password (Argon2id) and, if you turn it
on, your device's biometric hardware. Your master password and recovery key are never stored or sent anywhere.

## Backups
If you turn on Auto-backup, MavLock writes an encrypted copy of your vault to a file in a location
you choose (for example, Google Drive) using Android's file picker. The file cannot be read without
your master password or recovery key. MavLock itself never connects to the internet; any syncing is
done by the storage app you picked and is governed by that provider's privacy policy.

## Autofill
If you enable MavLock as your autofill service, Android shows MavLock the login fields of the app or
website you are using so that it can fill or save a login. This information is processed on your
device only and is never transmitted.

## Permissions
- Biometric: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face.
No other permissions are requested.

## Data deletion
Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored in it. Backup files you created are yours; delete them from where you saved them.

## Children
MavLock is not directed at children under 13.
